**(c) Clopper-Pearson intervals.** The exact intervals were in the stored CSVs (cp_lo, cp_hi) and the code asserts that the recomputed intervals equal them to 1e-6; they were not in the paper. SM Table S8 now lists hits and the exact interval for every Table III cell (285/300 is 91.9-97.2%, as ChatGPT says); Sec. II.C points to it and says the cells are not independent.

**(d) Lower betting-inversion grid.** `certified.py` skips a multiplier lambda_j = g_j/b_w for the lower test when lambda_j m >= 1 and divides by the full 18, not the admissible number: an inadmissible term contributes zero, so the statistic stays an average of non-negative test martingales each starting at most at 1 (Ville applies, nothing renormalised). Each term is continuous and non-increasing in the trial mean m (it falls to 0 as m -> 1/lambda_j), so the statistic is non-increasing and the 45-step bisection on log m finds its crossing. Checked on the stored d=9 draws (8 strata, 4000-point log grid each): no violation of monotonicity for the lower (non-increasing) or upper (non-decreasing) statistic (largest 0.0); the first cutoff m = b_w/30 = 0.11 is at least 7.7 times the largest bound computed (0.0147), so all 18 lower multipliers are admissible at every reported bound and the grid change does not touch the numbers; dense-grid roots agree with the bisection within 0.7% (grid step under 1%). The upper-test grid has g_j <= 0.999 and no cutoff. Added to SM Sec. S7.

**(e) Hernando de la Fuente, Majaniemi, van Nieuwenburg and Frohnert (arXiv:2610.26785).** Verified on the arXiv abstract page (v1 19 Oct 2026, v2 6 Nov 2026; title "Correlated decoding loses the surface code's effective distance below threshold"; authors as given): correlated surface-code decoders that reweight one decoding subgraph from the other can fail on fault patterns a distance-d code should correct. Abstract only, not read in full, and said so in the text. Cited in the related work and beside the remark on the leading power; it concerns a correlated decoder, not the PyMatching decoder used here.

**(f) Blind distance extrapolation.** Pre-registered in `PLAN.md` ("FINAL-CHECK AMENDMENT", item (f)), not run.
